Entry for **The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary** — one word, no explanation, just the word itself: `monosyllabic`.

`main.ts` writes that single word deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` (12 bytes, no trailing newline).

## Why this word

`monosyllabic` means "having one syllable" — but the word itself has five syllables: mo-no-syl-la-bic. It fails its own definition on sight, in its own spelling. Deleting it makes the dictionary slightly more honest, and the contradiction lands at first glance with no setup and no explanation — just the word staring back at you, five syllables long, claiming to be one.

## Rejected alternates

- **oversight** — a contronym (both "supervision" and "a mistake"), but the joke needs a moment of recall to land.
- **colonel** — spelling vs. pronunciation is funny, but it clusters with the already-saturated queue/mnemonic spelling-joke field.

The reasoning above lives only in this README. The entry itself is just the word.
